ABOUT BOOKSWORLD

A Better Place To Read Together.

BooksWorld is an interactive platform built for passionate readers. Discover great stories, share honest thoughts, and build book clubs that last.

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect book lovers worldwide, foster meaningful discussions, and help you manage your book clubs seamlessly.

Meet The Book Lover

There are so many social media apps to check out a movie or a book you love but it is very easy to get distracted by something else then before you know it you are doomscrolling. Then the idea hit, we can have TikTok but then for books and BooksWorld was born.

As a book lover I wanted a world of just books. Since I couldn’t find one I created it.

Welcome to my world of books I hope you love it as much as I loved creating it.

Our vision

The vision for BooksWorld is to create a community and interactive book environment that is safe and fun, making books accessible to anyone who loves to read and generally encouraging reading.
